Memorize Scripture with a guided course and practice that fits how you actually learn. Free, private, and works offline.

Four ways to practice

Fade

Hide more words each round until you can say the whole passage unaided.

First letters

See only the first letter of each word and recite from there.

Type it

Type from memory and see exactly which words you missed.

Recite aloud

Speak the passage and get it checked against the text.

A guided 12-module course

Not just a list of verses. Each module has a purpose, topics, and a short set of priority memory verses.

Twelve modules in one simple list: the gospel, Jesus Christ, understanding God, the Word, prayer, the Holy Spirit, faith, new life in Christ, church, spiritual warfare, Christian living, and mission.

Built to be used

Review reminders

Rate how well you know each passage and the app shows what's due.

Three translations built in

KJV, ASV and Bible in Basic English, plus your own pasted text for NIV, NLT or NKJV.

Private by design

No account. Your progress stays on your device.
