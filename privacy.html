Privacy Policy

Hid in my heart — Bible Memory Verse Trainer · Last updated: 28 October 2026

In short: the app itself collects no personal data. It has no accounts, cookies, ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting, and it loads nothing from other companies. Your progress stays in your browser on your own device. Like every website, the company that hosts the app (GitHub) records the IP address of each visit for security.

What is stored on your device

The app saves the following in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

WhatWhy
For each passage: your rating, best recall score, when you last practised it, and when it is next dueTo show your progress and what to review
Your favorite passagesFor the Favorites list
The dates you opened the app or practised (for example "2026-10-28: practised")For the Consistency calendar and streak
Preferences: theme, text size, Bible version, last passage and last viewSo the app opens the way you left it
A version number for the stored data, and a backup copy made once when the app upgrades its data formatSo updates never lose your progress

None of this includes your name, email, location or any device identifier. The app also keeps its own files (pages, Bible text, fonts, icons) in your browser's cache so it works offline.

Your control over your data

Open Consistency → Your data in the app to:

  • Export my data: download everything listed above as a JSON file.
  • Import data: restore an exported file, for example on a new device. Files are checked before anything is replaced.
  • Clear all data: delete it all from this browser, after asking you to confirm.

Because your data lives only in this browser, clearing your browser's site data, using a private window, or moving to another browser or device will lose it unless you export it first. We cannot recover it for you, because we never have a copy.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. We cannot see or switch off those logs, and GitHub does not publish how long it keeps them. GitHub Pages gives us no visitor analytics, and we have added none. See GitHub's note on data collection and GitHub's privacy statement.

Microphone and speech recognition

The "Recite" mode uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, and only after you press Start and give permission. The audio is processed by your browser's speech service, not by us. In Chrome and Edge this typically means the audio is sent to Google or Microsoft servers; in Safari it is handled by Apple. Their own privacy policies apply to that audio. We do not receive or store recordings or transcripts. If you prefer, use the Type it mode instead, which needs no microphone.

Fonts

The app's fonts (Newsreader and Instrument Sans) are stored on this site, so loading them contacts no other company.

Links to other sites

Each passage has links to read it in NIV, NLT and NKJV on BibleGateway. The app never downloads or shows text from BibleGateway. When you tap one of those links, the app tells you that you are leaving, and then opens BibleGateway in a new tab. The link contains only the passage reference and version, and your browser is told not to send the address of the page you came from. BibleGateway's own privacy policy applies there. If you choose to give through the donation link, Ko-fi handles the payment on its own site under its own privacy policy.

Bible text

The built-in text comes from public-domain translations: King James Version, American Standard Version (1901) and Bible in Basic English. Copyrighted translations are not included and cannot be added to the app.

Children

The app does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information, and stores nothing that identifies them. The hosting logs described above apply to every visitor.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws, such as the GDPR, you have rights over personal data held about you. The app holds none on our side: everything is on your device, where you can export or delete it yourself. For the IP addresses in GitHub's hosting logs, contact GitHub through its privacy statement above. You can also contact us with any privacy question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
