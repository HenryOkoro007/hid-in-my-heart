Privacy Policy

Hid in my heart — Bible Memory Verse Trainer · Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your progress and any text you paste stay on your own device. We don't collect, sell or share personal information.

What is stored on your device

The app saves the following in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • Your ratings and recall scores for each passage, and when you last practiced.
  • Any Bible text you paste in under "My own text".
  • Your preferences: theme, last passage, last view and chosen version.

This information is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

Microphone and speech recognition

The "Recite" mode uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, and only after you press Start and give permission. The audio is processed by your browser's speech service, not by us. In Chrome and Edge this typically means the audio is sent to Google or Microsoft servers; in Safari it is handled by Apple. Their own privacy policies apply to that audio. We do not receive or store recordings or transcripts. If you prefer, use the Type it mode instead, which needs no microphone.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts (Newsreader and Instrument Sans) from Google Fonts. When fonts are first loaded, your browser makes a request to Google, which can include your IP address. After the first load the fonts are cached, so the app can work offline.

Links to other sites

The app links to BibleGateway so you can read passages in NIV, NLT and NKJV. Those links open in a new tab and are governed by BibleGateway's own privacy policy. If you choose to support the project through a donation link, that payment is handled by the payment provider, not by this app.

Bible text

The built-in text comes from public-domain translations: King James Version, American Standard Version (1901) and Bible in Basic English. Copyrighted translations are not included.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
